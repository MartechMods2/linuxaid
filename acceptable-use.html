LinuxAid trust centre

Acceptable use

LinuxAid is for learning, troubleshooting and constructive community collaboration.

Do not

Do not use LinuxAid for credential theft, malware distribution, unauthorized access, harassment, doxxing, spam, illegal activity, deliberate service disruption or attempts to bypass access controls and rate limits.

Community conduct

Be specific, respectful and useful. Share enough technical context to help others without exposing personal information or secrets.

Enforcement

Content may be removed and accounts may be limited or suspended when needed to protect users, the service or other people.
