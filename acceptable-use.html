Acceptable use

Learn Linux without harming people or systems.

Last updated 17 September 2026.

Allowed

Learning Linux, practicing in the simulator, debugging systems you own or are authorized to administer, sharing legitimate fixes, discussing defensive security, collaborating on open-source projects and helping other learners.

Not allowed

Do not use LinuxAid to facilitate unauthorized access, credential theft, destructive malware, denial-of-service abuse, doxxing, harassment, spam, evasion of access controls, distribution of stolen data or deliberate compromise of systems you do not have permission to test.

Credentials and private data

Never publish passwords, API keys, session tokens, private keys, recovery codes or other people's private information. Redact logs before posting when they contain sensitive values.

Community moderation

Content may be reported and reviewed. Repeated abuse, spam or attempts to defeat platform safeguards can result in restricted access.

Security learning

Security tools are legitimate in authorized labs and defensive work. Permission and scope matter: use isolated labs, capture-the-flag environments or systems for which you have explicit authorization.
