Privacy

LinuxAid uses account and product data only to operate, secure and improve the service.

Data LinuxAid may process

This can include account identifiers, profile information you choose to provide, learning progress, community activity, messages, security events and optional consented product analytics.

Private conversations

Private messages are intended for conversation members. Do not use LinuxAid messages to transmit passwords, private keys, access tokens or other secrets.

Analytics

Optional product analytics can be controlled from your profile. Session replay is disabled. Where supported, browser privacy preferences are respected.

Security

LinuxAid uses access controls, authenticated requests, request limits and other safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so use strong unique passwords and keep credentials private.

Your choices

You can change profile visibility, appearance and analytics preferences from your profile, and export local progress where that option is available.
